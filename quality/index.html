How we process and check every coil

The route our strip takes through the Bawana works, what we work to at each step, and how coils are packed for the journey to you.

01Processing route

AI / PR / 04

The processing route

Annealing and cold rolling repeat until the strip reaches the thickness you ordered. Hardened and tempered strip then goes through hardening, quenching and tempering in an inert atmosphere before it is finished and edged.

  1. 01

    Raw material

    Hot rolled coil is received and slit to a workable width.

  2. 02

    Scale breaking and slitting

    Mill scale is cracked off mechanically and the coil is slit.

  3. 03

    Acid pickling

    Remaining scale is removed in acid baths, leaving clean steel.

  4. 04

    Annealing

    The strip is softened so it can be rolled further without cracking.

  5. 05

    Cold rolling

    Rolled at room temperature down to the thickness you order.

  6. 06

    Skin passing

    A light final pass sets the surface finish and flatness.

  7. 07

    Cold rolled slitting

    Slit to your width, with the edge you specify.

  8. 08

    Packing and dispatch

    Coils are sealed, hooped and wrapped for transport.

02What we work to
